Why I built normALL

I am neurodivergent and help clients use data and technology to answer complex questions in biopharma R&D. I built normALL because people who approach the same problem differently can help each other see solutions they might miss on their own.

Take a meeting: having time to organise your thoughts can help, and so can receiving the questions in advance or being able to follow up in writing. Some of those choices belong to the person taking part, while others belong to the person running the meeting.

This thinking shaped normALL’s four workspaces. I would like neurodivergent professionals to be able to choose the support that suits them, and colleagues, managers, and HR teams to have practical ways to make the workplace itself more inclusive.

Lavinia Woodward, founder of normALL
